# Calculation Assumptions

The prototype models a deliberately narrow and explainable scenario:

- tax year 2026;
- permanent private-sector employee;
- employee works for the full year;
- tax residence in Milan, Lombardy;
- one employer and no other income;
- no dependants or family deductions;
- no personal deductible expenses or tax credits;
- no bonuses, overtime, productivity awards or fringe benefits;
- no special tax regimes or contribution exemptions;
- gross annual salary excludes TFR;
- ordinary employee contribution rate estimated at 9.19%;
- annual net is divided evenly by the selected 12, 13 or 14 payments.

The result is an estimate, not a payroll calculation or professional tax advice. Actual payslips may distribute deductions, local surtaxes, thirteenth salary and year-end adjustments differently during the year.
